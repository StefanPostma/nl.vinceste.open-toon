Bring a Toon thermostat that runs the open software into Homey. Set the target temperature and follow the room temperature, the boiler and your electricity, gas and hot water in Insights and in your Flows. The app reads the Toon by itself, over your local network and without a cloud. It needs a Toon with the open software and an open firewall, it does not work with a Toon as it comes out of the box, and you use it at your own risk.

ToonWater, the add-on that makes your water readings available on the Toon, is optional and is switched on per device in the device settings. Turning ToonWater off removes the water items again: Flows that use them will break and their Insights history may be lost. Add every Toon only once, because two devices for the same Toon double the number of requests the Toon has to answer and are not supported. After you replace a meter, Homey Energy may show one odd interval, because the app only accepts a lower meter value after three readings in a row show it.
