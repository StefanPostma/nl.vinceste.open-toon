# ADR-0018: `store.toonwaterDecidedBy` - how the opt-in treats devices whose ToonWater state was decided by the migration, not by the user

* Status: accepted (2026-11-12, design revision rev 2); amends FR-54 AC2, FR-56 AC6 and FR-62 AC1 of `01-requirements.md`; corrects the premise of `04` R-08; owner decision on the warning rule (item 3) recorded with a one-flag reversal
* Related: `05-architecture.md` §6.3, §6.4, §6.6, §6.7, §9; FR-54, FR-56, FR-62, FR-74 AC4; `04` R-04, R-08, §7.3 condition 2, CD-2; ADR-0006, ADR-0007, ADR-0010; design reviews `archive/05a-design-review-compat.md` issues 2, 5, 6 and `archive/05a-design-review-robustness.md` R-14, R-22

## Context

The migration (ADR-0010 M-2) decides `toonwater_enabled` from the capability list: eras 0/1 become **off**, eras 2/3 become **on**. Three findings showed that the design treated these devices as if a user had made the choice:

1. **`update_water` on an era-0/1 device.** The baseline offers `update_water` to every `open-toon` device (`app.json:513-525`, filter `driver_id=open-toon`) and `getWater()` swallows every error (`device.js:171-190`; the `Invalid Capability` from `_processWaterData` lands in a `.catch` whose `if (err.error)` even hides the log line). Under the baseline the card therefore *silently resolved* on devices without water. Users who built the "timer Flow with all update cards" workaround (#5, #25) may have it chained. The design's `onDisabled: 'reject'` would make that Flow stop at the card after the upgrade alone, a Flow break without any user action. `04` R-08 accepted the rejection on the premise "reachable only after a deliberate user toggle; migrated devices are `enabled`", which is true only for eras 2/3.
2. **Warning on P1 devices.** Every device paired since PR #21 carries the water trio with or without the add-on. After the upgrade the migration sets them to **on**, and the first 404 would call `setWarning(...)`: a persistent banner on the tile of a probably large group, caused by the update itself, while FR-62 informs the same users once via the timeline. FR-56 AC6 asks for the warning, so this is a conscious choice that `05` had not surfaced to the owner.
3. **FR-62 restart safety.** Only `store.waterHintSent` was persisted. Whether the device was enabled by migration, and when the 404 streak began, lived in memory; the install itself restarts the app, so the one-time hint could fire late, never, or for the wrong device class.

## Decision

A persisted per-device flag records *who* decided the ToonWater state.

1. **Flag.** `store.toonwaterDecidedBy`: `'migration'` written by M-2 together with `toonwater_enabled`; `'user'` written by `buildNewDevice()` at pairing (ticked or not) and by every `onSettings` that changes `toonwater_enabled`. It is never cleared; a device that has no value (a fork build) is treated as `'user'`.
2. **`update_water` while off** (`cards.onDisabled`): when `toonwaterDecidedBy === 'migration'` the card **resolves as a no-op** (no request, no capability write, one debug line "update_water ignored: ToonWater is off for this device"); otherwise it rejects with the FR-59 text "ToonWater is not enabled for this device" / "ToonWater staat niet aan voor dit apparaat" and sends nothing. **FR-54 AC2 is amended** to "... **Given** ToonWater was disabled by the user ..."; FR-74 AC4 follows it. `04` R-08's premise is corrected accordingly. The one-word fallback `onDisabled: 'noop'` (CD-2) stays available.
3. **Warning rule** (owner decision; recommended default adopted): while enabled and `/water.html` is `not_found`, `setWarning(__('toonwater.warning_not_found'))` is called only when `toonwaterDecidedBy === 'user'` **or** any of the three water capabilities holds a non-`null` value (the add-on existed once, so a 404 is a real fault). For a migration-decided device whose three water values are all `null` (add-on never seen), the status label shows "Not found on this Toon (HTTP 404)" and the FR-62 timeline hint is sent after 24 h, but no tile warning is set. **FR-56 AC6 is amended** to "... behaves like FR-55 AC1 **except that the tile warning is replaced by the FR-62 hint** when the device never held a water value". The feature object carries `warnWhenMigrationDecided: false`; the owner reverts to "always warn" by flipping it to `true` (one word, covered by both L2 variants).
4. **FR-62 persistence.** The water group writes `store.waterFirst404At = now` on entering `not_found` when the key is absent, and deletes it on any valid payload and on any user toggle. The hint fires when `toonwaterDecidedBy === 'migration' && now - waterFirst404At >= 24 h && !store.waterHintSent`, is sent with `this.homey.notifications.createNotification({ excerpt })` (no manifest permission needed, [R] ManagerNotifications.html, verified by the standards review), and `store.waterHintSent = true` is written **only after the call resolved**; a rejected call is logged and retried at the next water attempt. **FR-62 AC1** gains "... the 24 h window and the migration origin survive app and Homey restarts (store keys)". FR-62 AC2/AC3 are unchanged (user-decided devices never get the hint; a disabled or deleted device clears/loses the key).
5. **Scope.** The flag changes nothing for user-decided devices: they keep `reject`, the warning and no hint, exactly as ADR-0006/0007 specified.

## Consequences

* Upgrading an era-0/1 device breaks no Flow: a chained `update_water` keeps resolving, as it did under the baseline, until the user makes a choice.
* Upgrading an era-2/3 device without the add-on produces no new tile warning; the user learns about the stale items once, via the timeline, and finds the switch in the device settings (FR-62). Cost: a P1 device keeps its empty water tiles with no visual cue on the tile until that hint arrives (risk 13 in `05` §16).
* Tests: CT-04 gains "era-1 fixture: `update_water` resolves, zero `/water.html` requests, one debug line"; L2 water-mode scenarios gain "migration-decided, all values `null`, 404: status set, no `setWarning`, hint at 24 h across a device re-instantiation over the same fake store at 12 h" and "migration-decided with a historical value, 404: `setWarning`"; "createNotification rejects once: no `waterHintSent`, retried, sent once".
* Rejected: `onDisabled: 'noop'` for everyone (hides a genuine configuration mistake from a user who disabled ToonWater and still runs the card); always warning (update-caused noise on the largest user group, against the owner's "no 404 noise" intent); an in-memory origin flag (not restart-safe).
